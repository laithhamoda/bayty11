What is Bayty?

Bayty is construction management software for project teams that need one secure place to manage procurement, document control, approvals, communication, risk and delivery visibility.

The platform is built for construction owners, real estate developers, general contractors, consultants, suppliers and project management offices that work across complex projects and international teams.

Instead of separating RFQs, drawings, submittals, contracts, decisions and executive reporting across disconnected tools, Bayty connects them into a single operating layer for construction project delivery.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025 | Last updated: 1 January 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Bayty Global collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when visitors use bayty.com, request a demo, communicate with the team or access Bayty services. This policy is designed for GDPR and UK GDPR readiness and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before production launch.

Information we collect

Bayty may collect identity and contact data, including name, work email, phone number, job title, company, country, role, project volume and any information submitted through demo, support or sales forms. When platform accounts are enabled, Bayty may also process authentication records, profile settings and team membership data.

Project and usage data

Bayty may process project metadata, procurement records, document names, approval status, RFQ activity, audit logs, device information, browser data, approximate location, consent records and usage events needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

How we use information

Information is used to respond to demo requests, provide the website and platform, manage accounts, deliver support, send transactional messages, improve product performance, measure marketing effectiveness, prevent misuse, comply with legal obligations and communicate with authorized business contacts.

Legal bases for processing

Bayty processes personal data where necessary to perform a contract, pursue legitimate business interests, comply with legal obligations, protect the service, or where consent has been provided for optional analytics and marketing activities.

Data protection

Bayty applies role-based access, audit controls, operational safeguards, data minimization, encryption where appropriate, secure vendor review and internal access controls aligned with enterprise software expectations.

Cookies and analytics

Necessary cookies support core site functionality. Optional analytics cookies are loaded only after consent and may be used to understand traffic, conversion events, pricing-page engagement and demo-form performance. Users may reject optional cookies without losing access to the website.

Sharing and processors

Bayty may share data with trusted service providers that support hosting, CRM, analytics, email delivery, authentication, security monitoring and customer support. These processors may only use data according to Bayty instructions and applicable data protection obligations.

International transfers

Bayty is designed for global project teams. Personal data may be processed in countries outside the user's location. Where required, Bayty will use appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections or recognized transfer mechanisms.

Data retention

Bayty retains personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including service delivery, legal compliance, security, auditability, dispute resolution and legitimate business records. Demo and sales records are periodically reviewed for relevance.

Your rights

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Requests can be submitted through the contact route below.

Contact and data protection lead

For privacy questions or data subject requests, contact Bayty at privacy@bayty.com. Before launch, Bayty should confirm its legal entity details, postal address and appointed privacy contact or data protection officer where required.
